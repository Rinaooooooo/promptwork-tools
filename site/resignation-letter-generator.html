FAQ

How much notice should I give?

Follow your contract and local norms — two to four weeks is typical. State one clear last working day and stick to it.

Should I explain my real reasons in detail?

No. One neutral line is enough. Gratitude plus a clean handover offer protects your reputation far better than a full explanation.
