FAQ

What are the parts of an apology that actually works?

Four: name the specific failure, take responsibility without excuses, state what you fixed, and say how it will not repeat. Skip any one of them and the apology feels hollow.

Is "sorry if you were offended" ever acceptable?

No. It apologizes for the other person's feelings instead of your actions, and everyone reads it that way. If you are not ready to own the action, wait until you are.
