About PromptWork Tools

PromptWork Tools is a small, independent collection of free office tools: weekly reports, meeting minutes, emails, Excel formulas, presentation outlines and the paperwork of working life — cover letters, resignation letters, performance reviews and more.

How the tools work

Every tool works the same way: you fill in a short form, and the tool structures your material instantly, using rules that run 100% in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored. Each tool also gives you a copy-ready AI prompt, so if you want a smarter rewrite you can paste it into ChatGPT or Claude yourself — your choice, your data.

Why free?

Because the tools run on your device, they cost almost nothing to offer. There are no accounts and no paywalls. If a tool saves you time, the best thank-you is to bookmark it and share it with a coworker.

Who runs this?

An independent creator building practical tools for office workers, one small tool at a time. New tools are added based on what people actually search for and ask about.
