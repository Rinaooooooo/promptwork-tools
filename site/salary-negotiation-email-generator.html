FAQ

When is the best moment to send this?

Right after a visible win — a shipped project, a closed deal, a glowing review — and never in the middle of a crisis or a conflict. Timing is half the negotiation.

Should the first ask happen by email or in person?

Email first, meeting second. A written ask gives your manager something concrete to take to their own boss; the meeting is where you discuss it, not where they first hear it.
