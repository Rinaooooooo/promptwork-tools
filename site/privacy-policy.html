Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

The short version

PromptWork Tools runs entirely in your browser. The text you type into any tool on this site is processed on your own device and is never uploaded to, or stored on, a server by us. We have no user accounts, no sign-up, and no database of visitor content.

What we collect

Tool inputs: nothing. Everything you enter into a generator stays in the page on your device. When you close the tab, it is gone.

Basic hosting logs. This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like any web host, Cloudflare may process standard technical information (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. That processing is governed by Cloudflare's own privacy policy.

Cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. If this changes in the future — for example, if we add advertising — this policy will be updated before the change takes effect, and any consent requirements that apply will be honoured.

Third parties

Some tools offer a copy-ready prompt that you may choose to paste into a third-party AI service such as ChatGPT or Claude. That action is entirely yours: nothing is sent anywhere until you paste it yourself, and whatever you share there is covered by that service's privacy policy, not this one.

Children

This site provides general office productivity tools and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the About & Contact page.
