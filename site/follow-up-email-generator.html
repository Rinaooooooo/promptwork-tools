FAQ

How fast should a thank-you email go out?

Within 24 hours, while the conversation is still fresh for both sides. Same day is best; after two days it starts to read as an afterthought.

How many nudges are too many?

Two, spaced about a week apart. After that, silence is the answer — a third nudge only spends goodwill.
