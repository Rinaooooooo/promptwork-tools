FAQ

What belongs in a resume summary?

Role, years, industry, 3-4 core strengths and your single strongest measurable result. Nothing else — it is a headline, not a biography.

Summary or objective statement?

Summary. Objective statements ("seeking a challenging role") describe what you want; summaries describe what the employer gets.
