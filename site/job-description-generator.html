FAQ

Why do long requirement lists hurt hiring?

Every "must-have" filters out candidates who would have been great. Research on job ads consistently shows qualified people self-select out when they meet only part of an inflated list. Keep must-haves to what would truly disqualify.

Should I include the salary range?

Yes, if you can. Postings with a range get more and better-matched applicants, and in a growing number of places it is legally required.
