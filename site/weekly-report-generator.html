FAQ

How do I write a weekly report that is not just a list?

Write every item as Action + Task + Result. "Published 2 posts (12k views)" tells your manager what happened; "worked on posts" does not.

Should I include blockers?

Yes — a blocker with your next step ("waiting on a quote, following up Monday") shows progress management, not excuses.
