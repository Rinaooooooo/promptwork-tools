FAQ

How long should a cover letter be?

250-350 words. Hiring managers skim: one role fit, one proof point, one genuine reason for this company.

Should I just repeat my resume?

No. The letter explains why you fit this specific role; the resume lists what you have done. The generated AI prompt enforces that split.
