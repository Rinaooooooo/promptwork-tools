FAQ

What makes a LinkedIn headline work?

Searchability. Recruiters type role titles and skills, not slogans. "Operations Manager | Supply Chain Planning | E-commerce Logistics" gets found; "Passionate problem-solver" does not.

First person or third person for the About section?

First person. It reads as a human talking, and it is the voice people expect when they reach out to connect.
