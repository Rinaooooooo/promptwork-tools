FAQ

What do managers actually look for in a self-review?

Evidence. Every claim should carry a number, a date or a before-and-after. "Improved the process" is forgettable; "cut resolution time from 9h to 5h" is a calibration anchor.

Should I hide my weak areas?

No — name one real challenge and what you changed because of it. A review with no weakness reads as either unaware or dishonest, and managers discount the whole document.
